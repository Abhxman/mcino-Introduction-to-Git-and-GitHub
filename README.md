# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator that calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period in years.

## Inputs

- **Principal (p):** The initial amount of money.
- **Rate (r):** The annual rate of interest in percentage.
- **Time (t):** The time period in years.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Example

For a principal of 1000, an annual interest rate of 5%, and a time period of 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

## Output

The calculator displays the calculated simple interest.
